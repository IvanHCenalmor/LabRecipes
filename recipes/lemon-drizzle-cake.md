# Lemon Drizzle Cake

[← Back to Recipe Index](../README.md#recipe-index)

**Shared by:** Guillaume
**Nationality:** French

## The Protocol
### Ingredients
* 175g butter or margarine
* 175g caster sugar
* 175g self-raising flour, sieved
* 3 large eggs, beaten
* Grated zest of 2 lemons

#### Topping
* 75g caster sugar
* Juice of 2–3 lemons (depending on size and juiciness)

### Methods
1. Pre-heat oven to gas mark 3 (170°C), and grease and line a deep cake tin 18–20cm in diameter.
2. Mix together butter (or margarine) and sugar until light and creamy.
3. Add the eggs a little at a time, beating well.
4. Gradually fold in the flour and lemon zest.
5. Pour the mixture into the tin and bake for about 1 hour, until golden and firm to the touch.
6. As soon as the cake goes in the oven, mix together the sugar and lemon juice so the sugar has time to dissolve.
7. Mix well again before pouring over the cake.
8. When the cake is cooked, turn it out of the tin and place it on a wire rack with a large plate underneath.
9. While the cake is still hot, pierce it all over with a thin-bladed knife and gradually spoon the lemon-sugar mix over, allowing the juice to soak in between each addition.
10. Let any excess drip into the plate, and allow the cake to cool before serving.
